About Hall Road Lahore — G Power & Electronics

We are G Power & Electronics, located at Shop No. 1, Sarwar Centre, Hall Road, Lahore — Pakistan's largest electronics market. We sell the same genuine stock we sell over the counter, at Hall Road wholesale prices, delivered to your door nationwide.

What we sell

We focus on power backup and solar, because that is what Hall Road is known for and where we can genuinely advise you:

  • Direct PV solar inverters (no battery needed) — Energy 1.5KW (1–3 panels) and Simko 3.2KW Black Panther (2–4 panels) pure sine wave VFD inverters.
  • MPPT solar charge controllers — Simtek Planet40 (40A), Simtek DC King (70A), Energy MPPT (80A), Simtek MPPT+ Hybrid 85A and 120A.
  • 12V power inverters — Alkaram 2000W, 3000W, 4000W and 6000W DC-to-AC power inverters for home and shop backup.
  • Smart battery chargers — Simtek 12V 20A, 20A Digital Display, and 30A heavy-duty automatic chargers with full-charge auto cut-off.
  • Water heaters & solar accessories — 3000W instant electric water heater taps with LED temperature display, plus stainless steel and plastic solar panel water drain clips.

Why customers order from us

  • Hall Road pricing. We buy at wholesale and pass the difference on. Our "was" prices are real market prices — we do not inflate them to fake a discount.
  • Cash on Delivery nationwide. You pay the rider when the parcel reaches you. No advance needed for most orders.
  • Warranty is stated per product. Every product page lists the exact warranty you get, rather than one vague site-wide promise. See Returns & warranty.
  • Real advice. Message us your load (which appliances, how many hours) and we will tell you the right inverter and battery size — even if it means a cheaper unit. Try the solar calculator first.

How ordering works

  1. Add products to your cart and tap Proceed to Checkout.
  2. Fill in your name, WhatsApp number, city and address. Choose COD or advance payment.
  3. Your order opens in WhatsApp with an order ID. Send it — that confirms the order.
  4. We confirm stock and delivery time on WhatsApp, usually within working hours.
  5. The courier calls you before delivery. Pay cash on delivery.
Business hours: Monday to Saturday, 9:00am to 5:00pm (PKT). Orders placed on WhatsApp outside these hours are confirmed the next working morning.
