    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do you deliver to my city? How much is delivery?

    Yes - we deliver nationwide via courier. Lahore is Rs. 150 (same/next day), Punjab Rs. 200, Sindh/KPK/Balochistan Rs. 250 and AJK/Gilgit-Baltistan Rs. 350. Orders are dispatched within 24 hours (Mon-Sat).

    How do I place an order?

    Add products to the cart, tap "Proceed to Checkout" and fill the form - your order opens in WhatsApp ready to send. No account needed. You can also order any product directly from its WhatsApp button.

    What payment methods do you accept?

    Cash on Delivery (COD) anywhere in Pakistan, or 30% advance via JazzCash / EasyPaisa to confirm the order - the rest is paid on delivery.

    Are these products original? What warranty do I get?

    Yes - we sell only original Simtek and Alkaram products sourced from Hall Road, Lahore. Every product shows its exact warranty on the product page (3-12 months on Simtek, 6 months shop warranty on Alkaram inverters).

    Can I return a product if it has a problem?

    Yes - you have 7 days. Manufacturing defects are collected and returned at our cost; change-of-mind returns are accepted unused in original packaging with courier paid by the customer. See the returns policy.

    Which MPPT controller should I choose - 40A, 70A, 85A or 120A?

    It depends on your panel wattage and battery bank: 40A for small 12V setups (up to ~500W panels), 70A for 12V/24V systems up to ~1000W, 85A for larger 12V/24V arrays with 150V panel input, and 120A for the biggest home setups. Ask us on WhatsApp and we will size it free: use the solar calculator.

    What is the difference between a modified sine wave and pure sine wave inverter?

    Modified sine wave (Alkaram PI series) runs fans, lights, TVs, chargers and most tools at a much lower price. Pure sine wave is needed for sensitive electronics, inverter ACs and some refrigerators. Read our full guide.
