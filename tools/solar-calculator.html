How the numbers work

  • Inverter size — your total connected load plus 25% headroom, so the inverter is not running at its limit. Motors (fridges, pumps, AC compressors) draw 3-7x their running wattage for a second at startup, which is why headroom matters.
  • Battery capacity — daily energy use divided by the system voltage, then doubled because lead-acid batteries should not be discharged below 50%, then divided by 80% inverter efficiency.
  • Solar array — daily energy use divided by your city's peak sun hours, then increased by 25% to cover cloudy days, dust on panels and cable losses.
